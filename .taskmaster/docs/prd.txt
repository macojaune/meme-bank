# Product Requirement Document (PRD)

## Project Overview

### Title
Video Meme Archive for the Caribbean

### Purpose
To create a web platform that allows users to browse, share, and archive video memes specific to Guadeloupe, Martinique, and the Caribbean. The platform will utilize AI for transcription, categorization, and tagging to enhance user experience and content discoverability.

### Goals
- Provide a user-friendly interface for browsing and sharing video memes.
- Implement AI features for transcription, categorization, and tagging.
- Ensure legal compliance regarding image rights and copyrights.
- Create a mechanism for reporting and removing content.
- Build a community-driven platform that serves as a video archive.

## Target Audience
- Residents of Guadeloupe, Martinique, and the Caribbean.
- Social media users interested in local memes.
- Content creators looking for local video memes to integrate into their work.

## Business Model
- **Lifetime Access Fee**: A small one-time payment for lifetime access to the platform.
- **Purpose**: Fees are solely to cover maintenance costs of the platform, not to generate substantial profit.
- **Content Ownership**: Users retain full ownership of their content. The platform does not claim ownership or rights to sell user content.
- **Platform Access**: The fee is for access to the platform's features and services only.
- **Legal Compliance**: Legal consultation will be sought before launch to ensure all aspects of the business model comply with relevant laws and regulations.

## Features

### User Features
1. **User Registration and Authentication**
   - Users can create accounts and log in using email or social media accounts.
   - Password recovery and account management.

2. **Video Uploading**
   - Users can upload video memes.
   - Support for various video formats (e.g., MP4, AVI).

3. **AI Transcription and Tagging**
   - Automatically transcribe video content.
   - Generate tags based on the content and context of the video.

4. **Search Functionality**
   - Users can search for videos using quotes or keywords.
   - Filter results by categories, tags, and popularity.

5. **Browsing and Sharing**
   - Users can browse videos by categories (e.g., humor, culture).
   - Options to share videos directly to social media platforms.

6. **Reporting and Moderation**
   - Users can report inappropriate content.
   - Admins can review reports and remove content as necessary.

### Admin Features
1. **Content Management**
   - Admin dashboard to manage uploaded videos.
   - Tools for reviewing reports and moderating content.

2. **Analytics and Insights**
   - Track user engagement and video performance.
   - Generate reports on popular content and user activity.

3. **Legal Compliance**
   - Maintain a log of reported content and actions taken.
   - Implement a system for handling copyright claims.

## Technical Requirements

### Technology Stack
- **Backend Framework**: AdonisJS
- **Frontend Framework**: React
- **Language**: TypeScript
- **Database**: PostgreSQL or other database with vector capabilities
- **AI Services**: Integration with AI transcription and tagging services (e.g., Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, AWS Transcribe).
- **CDN Service**: Content Delivery Network for efficient video content delivery and global distribution (e.g., Cloudflare, AWS CloudFront, or Akamai).

### Performance Requirements
- Fast loading times for video content.
- Efficient database queries for search functionality.
- Scalable architecture to handle increasing user traffic.

### Security Requirements
- Secure user authentication and data storage.
- Implement measures to prevent unauthorized access and data breaches.
- Regular security audits and updates.

## Legal Considerations
- Ensure compliance with copyright laws and image rights.
- Implement a clear policy for content reporting and removal.
- Provide users with guidelines on acceptable content.
- Consult with legal professionals regarding the lifetime access fee model and content rights.

## Milestones
1. **Phase 1**: Research and Planning
   - Finalize feature set and technology stack.
   - Conduct market research and user surveys.
   - Consult with legal professionals on business model and content rights.

2. **Phase 2**: Development
   - Set up the development environment.
   - Implement user registration and authentication.
   - Develop video uploading and AI features.
   - Implement payment system for lifetime access.

3. **Phase 3**: Testing
   - Conduct user testing and gather feedback.
   - Fix bugs and optimize performance.
   - Test payment processing and access control.

4. **Phase 4**: Launch
   - Deploy the platform.
   - Implement marketing strategies to attract users.

5. **Phase 5**: Post-Launch
   - Monitor user engagement and gather feedback for improvements.
   - Plan for future updates and feature enhancements.
   - Evaluate maintenance costs and adjust lifetime fee if necessary.

## Conclusion
This PRD outlines the vision and requirements for the Video Meme Archive for the Caribbean. The project aims to create a valuable resource for the community while ensuring legal compliance and a positive user experience. The business model focuses on sustainability through a small lifetime access fee rather than monetizing user content.
